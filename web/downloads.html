Order a board

  1. Upload tandem-gerbers.zip to a fab (4 layers, 1.6 mm, 33 × 17.4 mm).
  2. For assembly, upload bom.csv and pos.csv from the assembly bundle; add LCSC part numbers.
  3. Print case-tray.stl and case-lid.stl in PETG, 0.2 mm layers, no supports. The lid is already oriented flat-side down.
  4. Hand-solder the USB-A plug (J1).

Flash: short TP1 (BOOT) to TP2 (GND) while plugging in, then flash over native USB.
