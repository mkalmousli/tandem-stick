Tools and materials

To order

Board from a PCB fab (4 layers, 1.6 mm). Parts from your usual supplier. Optional: assembly service for the small parts.

To solder

Hot air or reflow for the module and regulator, fine-tip iron for the plug, flux, tweezers, magnification.

To print

Any FDM printer with a 0.4 mm nozzle and PETG. Two small parts, no supports.

To flash

A computer with a USB port. Firmware is not released yet, so this step has to wait.

Build guide

  1. Check the files. Open the board in KiCad, look at the Gerbers in your fab's online viewer, and compare the board with the 3D explorer.
  2. Order the board. Upload tandem-gerbers.zip. Choose 4 layers, 1.6 mm, and order the minimum quantity first.
  3. Order the parts from the parts list. Order a few spare passives.
  4. Print the case. Tray and lid, PETG, settings below. Test-fit the lid before you solder anything.
  5. Solder the small parts. Regulator, LED and passives first, then the ESP32 module.
  6. Solder the USB-A plug last. Tack one pad, check it sits square, then solder the rest and the two shield tabs.
  7. Look it over. Under magnification, check for bridges. Use a multimeter to confirm 3V3 is not shorted to GND before power.
  8. Fit the case. Board into the tray on its ribs, plug through the slot, lid snaps on.
