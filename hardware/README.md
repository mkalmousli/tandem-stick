# Tandem Stick: board

KiCad 7 board generated by `build_board.py` (pcbnew API): `python3 build_board.py && python3 drc.py report.txt`.

- 33 × 17.4 mm main board plus a 13.5 mm USB-A tab (46.5 mm overall), 4 layers (signal, GND, 3V3, GND), 1.6 mm.
- `fab/gerbers/` Gerbers and drill, `fab/bom.csv`, `fab/pos.csv` for assembly. J1 is the board's own USB-A tab and TP1/TP2 are test pads, so none of them are in the BOM. Order ENIG (gold) finish for the plug pads.
- Fill the LCSC column and confirm stock and rotation offsets in the fab's preview before ordering.

## Flash
Short TP1 (BOOT) to TP2 (GND) while plugging in, then flash over USB (ESP32-S3 native USB).

## Known limits (not tested on hardware)
- DRC: 0 unconnected pads; only library-path and courtyard warnings remain (C1, C2 and U2 courtyards touch). No schematic or ERC; nets are in `build_board.py`.
- No ESD diode (planned for the respin). C3 (100 nF) sits on the back side under the module power pads, connected by via-in-pad: ask the fab for filled or tented vias, or accept the small solder-wicking risk.
- LED runs from 3.3 V and may be dim.
- Antenna end (x < 5.9 mm) is keepout on all layers; keep the case and your hand away from it.
- First article may need a respin. Order the minimum quantity first.
